MOOZHIO & OUR FACTORY

Built around the way bespoke millwork is made.

MOOZHIO delivers high-end bespoke millwork for overseas designers, architects, builders and contractors. Our role is to translate design intent into production-ready details and dependable delivery.

Our manufacturing focus is solid wood, veneer and high-quality painted finishes. From material review and machining to finishing, pre-assembly and final inspection, key stages are coordinated under a controlled production system.

MANUFACTURING EXPERTISE

Made for demanding woodwork specifications.

Our manufacturing expertise is centred on solid wood, natural veneer and premium painted finishes for bespoke interiors. Skilled handwork is combined with precision machining, controlled surface preparation and disciplined finishing to achieve consistent details across one-off pieces and project-scale packages.

We coordinate material selection, grain direction, joinery, color development and finish review against approved drawings and samples, helping designers and contractors move from specification to production with fewer surprises.
