Jiuli Qingchuan Villa

Villa · 800 m² · Romantic French
French elegance, modern restraint.An 800 m² villa in Shijiazhuang, composed through white timber panelling, grey and green marble and delicately moulded wall profiles — a romantic language held to a clean contemporary line.

Design & Details

White, stone and a red ribbon.
