Xinzhi Yuanjing Villa

Villa · 350 m² · Modern Minimalist
Shifting tranquility, made for a family.A 350 m² lower-duplex villa in Ningbo, organised around a sculptural curved stair, shared reading spaces and rooms designed to change as the family grows.
TypeVilla
LocationNingbo, Zhejiang, China
Area350 m²

Design & Details

Light, play and quiet order.

The open living room is left deliberately white, allowing material and structure to speak. A pegboard wall for ski gear, a dance room and pocket-door daughters’ suites give each member a place of their own, all held together by warm oak and soft lacquer.
