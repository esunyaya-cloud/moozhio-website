Our Story

Refined to perfection.

Ningbo Moozhio Co., Ltd. is a one-stop timber solutions provider integrating bespoke millwork, original design, on-site installation, supply chain management and procurement sourcing into a seamless, end-to-end service.

We specialise in high-end bespoke millwork, delivering fully engineered timber solutions — from design development and factory production through to quality assurance and final handover. Whether for luxury residences, boutique hotels or premium commercial spaces, we uphold the designer's original vision with precision, balancing structural integrity with refined aesthetics.
