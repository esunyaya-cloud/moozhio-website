Craft & Advantage

Why Professionals Choose MOOZHIO

From design development and material selection to manufacturing, pre-assembly and global delivery, we turn complex design intent into buildable, installable millwork.

01

1:1 3D Design & Engineering

We develop 1:1 spatial 3D models from client drawings and translate them into production-ready modular solutions, considering dimensions, structure, installation and overseas delivery from the start.

02

Extensive Material Library

An extensive library of veneers, panels, finishes, stone, glass, metal, hardware and lighting supports efficient and consistent material selection.

03

Two Owned Factories

Two owned factories integrate precision machining, craftsmanship, finishing and assembly under one coordinated production system.

04

QC & 1:1 Pre-assembly

Multi-stage quality control and 1:1 factory pre-assembly help identify dimensional, structural and installation issues before shipment.

05

Export-Ready Delivery

From component labeling and export packaging to logistics and customs documentation, every project is prepared for international delivery.

06

Installation Support

Installation drawings, component labeling and remote technical support help local teams execute projects accurately and efficiently.
