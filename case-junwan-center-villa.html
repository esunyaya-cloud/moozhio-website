Junwan Center Villa

Villa · 226 m² · Modern Minimalist
A free home above the river.A 226 m² duplex villa in Ningbo, shaped by a double-height living room, first-line river views and a low-saturation palette of grey and white — an exercise in less is more.
TypeVilla
LocationNingbo, Zhejiang, China

Design & Details

Less, but more precise.

Folded wall planes and layered volumes draw on Carlo Scarpa’s language of detail, while black frames outline each space. Concealed doors keep the bedrooms whole, and full-height glazing carries light and landscape deep into the plan.
