Wuling Yuefu Villa

Villa · 270 m² · Modern Minimalist × Oriental Zen
A holiday villa between East and West.A 270 m² villa at Xikou, Ningbo, reworked around larger glazing, a warm beige envelope and a raised wraparound entry — a modern reading of a traditional courtyard house.
TypeVilla
LocationNingbo, Zhejiang, China
Area270 m²
StyleModern Minimalist × Oriental Zen
MaterialsMoozhio Bespoke Walnut · Moozhio French-Italian White Lacquer

Design & Details

Light, ease and terracotta.

An open kitchen and dining room replace the original partitions, terracotta accents warm the suspended cabinetry, and a planted light well brings daylight to the basement tea room. Upstairs, a tatami room and a sloped master suite keep the mood unhurried.
